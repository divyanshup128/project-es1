    File a case: the procedure

    Pick the kind of problem to see where to file, by when, what to bring and what happens next. The examples use the same situations as the calculators and problem solver.

    1. Gather proofPayslips, letters, messages and bank entries.
    2. Ask in writingA grievance email first, then a demand notice with 15 days to pay.
    3. Pick the forumCentral or State government, and workman or not.
    4. Try conciliationFree, quick and often settles the matter.
    5. File your claimA statement of claim with the relief and documents.
    6. Attend hearingsEvidence, cross-examination and arguments.
    7. Get the orderAn award, a direction or a recovery certificate.
    8. Enforce or appealRecovery through the Collector, or a High Court writ.
